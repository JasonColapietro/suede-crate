      PrivacyUse with an assistant
      Your data

      Artist searches, selected Qloo IDs, and exclusions are sent through our server to Qloo. No account or listening history is needed. Your selections stay in this tab. Server responses may be privately cached for five minutes. Hosting and Qloo may retain operational logs under their policies. No analytics or advertising scripts are included.

      Connect an assistant

      Add this site’s /api/mcp URL to an MCP client that supports Streamable HTTP. It provides search_artists and build_music_crate. Requests use the same real Qloo data as this page. No client-side API key is needed.
